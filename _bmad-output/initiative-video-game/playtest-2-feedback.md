# Playtest 2 feedback (2026-10-07)

## Quick fixes: story 1.24 (in progress)

- **Two-way screens:** fix screens that don't connect in both directions.
- **Start screen:** the run starts on the Town face's centre screen, with townsfolk around.
- **"Entropy" in text:** allowed. The user will pick an in-game term later.
- **Floors:** more floor texture, and a calmer blue (Weightlands*) floor.
- **Fake items:** no decor that looks collectible (e.g. the key in the Wildlands*).
- **Math bridges:** bridges join real islands over water or crevices and are spaced apart. Some spans are broken by one square, which the Gap-Crosser* crosses.

## Playtest of 1.24 (2026-10-07)

- **Screens connecting both ways:**
  - The turned-input fix was rejected.
  - It is replaced by walls on every screen edge, with a centred doorway on each side.
  - Face-edge doorways are distinct gate arches.
  - Crossing a face edge stops movement until a fresh key press. This is story 1.25.
- **Entropy, floors and fake items:** good or better.
- **Math:**
  - The rework was still not right.
  - The design is now island screens: solid water, doorways on different islands, and spread-out bridges, so you plan how you get across the screen. This is story 1.25.
- **Towns (for the course correction):**
  - distinctive building designs, buildings you can enter, statues;
  - more people than on other screens, and no enemies;
  - one leader per town who gives a quest to help that region.

## Bigger changes for a course correction (discuss with the user)

1. **Edges and wrapping.**
   - The user says: "figure out a better solution to the wrapping since it isn't a flat map".
   - Proposal: smaller edges, where you enter a screen through a gate or door on a face edge (possibly everywhere).
   - Edge gates start locked and need certain equipment.
   - Each edge can have a different gate, giving several ways into the world.
2. **A town in the middle of every face.** Each face gets a town with NPCs on its centre square.
3. **A temple per face.** Each face gets a temple with an item to collect, Zelda-style. The item gives a benefit against the boss.
4. **Every screen has something.** Each screen has a puzzle to reach the next screen, enemies to fight, or an object to collect.
5. **More and harder puzzles.** More puzzles overall, and more complicated ones.
6. **Whole-screen weather.** Ice makes the whole screen slippery, wind causes gusts across it, and so on.
7. **Characters.**
   - Move the swappable body parts to the next epic.
   - Make nice-looking characters in a style similar to the main character.
8. **Scale (to discuss).** Bigger screens and bigger people together.
9. **Population is fine.** The user doesn't feel it is over-populated.
