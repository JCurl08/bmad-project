# Lore and Tone

## Lore
- Long ago, every race lived mixed together. **The Partition**, the doing of the Demon (inspired by Maxwell's Demon; never named that in game), split them onto separate faces.
- The Town face's town, built in ancient ruins that mix every face's style, is the last unpartitioned place. It is busy, mismatched and friendly. Every other face has a town of its own race, led by a leader who can repair the face once its fragments are brought back.
- Underground beneath the core, the demon's lair holds trapped people who whisper hints about the Demon and the repairs.
- The player is the embodiment of entropy and fights to undo the Partition. Entropy is the *positive* force: mixing means living together, and order means separation.
- The world's arc runs from order to chaos. The cube starts out ordered, like a solved Rubik's cube, and grows more chaotic as the player wins.

## Voice rules
- Campy and lighthearted, pitched at *Ocarina of Time*'s audience (pre-teens to adults). Silly, never explicit.
- Never say "sort" in-game. Show the demon's order through abstract imagery: a solved cube, aligned colours, stillness, separation.
- Comedy comes from each race's culture and manner of speech (voices in `faces-and-races.md`), grudges, puns (skeleton-key fingers), each race's earnest devotion to its patron figure and, in the Should tier, mismatched NPC parts.
- **Subtle science:** characters never know they stand for a science. In-world text never names a real scientist and never uses explicit science vocabulary for a face's theme (e.g. isotope, radioactive, relativity, evolution, niche, element). The analogy lives in mechanics and visuals only.
- Each race's patron figure (inspired by Darwin, Curie, Einstein, Euler and Anning/Richter) appears only under an in-world name, never the scientist's.
- **Names:** every character (townsfolk, leaders, lair prisoners, patron figures) gets a meaningful name in its race's culture, with no `*`. Region and item names keep a trailing `*` (e.g. "Weightlands*", "Gap-Crosser*") until the naming brainstorm replaces them.
- The more chaotic a seed, the more chaotic NPC dialogue gets. Hints stay present, but become cryptic.

## Cameos and gags (optional)
