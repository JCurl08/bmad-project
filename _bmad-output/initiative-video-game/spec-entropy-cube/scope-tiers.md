# Scope Tiers

Build order: epic-playable-cube, then epic-world-worth-exploring, then epic-varied-runs, then epic-stretch.

| Tier | Capabilities |
|---|---|
| Must (epic-playable-cube) | CAP-1 to CAP-8, CAP-10 to CAP-13, CAP-25 to CAP-27. All 6 faces at 3×3, every science face built, a cube-net map, and an arena exit. Ships as the playable baseline. |
| Must (epic-world-worth-exploring) | The rewritten CAP-2, CAP-7, CAP-8, CAP-11 and CAP-13, plus CAP-28 to CAP-32: bigger screens, locked edge gates, towns and leaders, fragments and repairs, roadblock rewards, ice and wind puzzles, tuned island puzzles, the lair, race cultures and names. |
| Should (epic-varied-runs) | CAP-9 and CAP-14 to CAP-17. Keep designs simple. |
| Could (epic-stretch) | CAP-18 to CAP-24 (CAP-24 run currency is cut for now) |

## Checkpoints
| Gate | Check |
|---|---|
| After the seeded cube story | The same seed gives the same cube, and a new seed gives a different one. Nothing else proceeds until this works. |
| After the Biology face | The vertical slice works end to end. Use its build time to re-plan the other two faces. |
| After the core boss | The boss works. If not, fall back to a simpler boss. |
| epic-playable-cube close | 3+ seeds play start to finish, and the build is published on itch.io. |
| After the puzzle research spike | Difficulty targets exist for every puzzle generator before the puzzle stories start. |
| User playtests in epic-world-worth-exploring | After the towns, after the locked edge gates, and after the lair. |
| epic-world-worth-exploring close | The sweep passes 50/50 seeds, the boss skill check holds at 60–120 s with no benefits, and the build is on itch.io. |

## Cut order (Should tier, drop first, top to bottom)
1. Multiple demon victories (CAP-15)
2. Face-based demon phases (CAP-15). Keep the single phase.
3. Puzzle difficulty scaling (CAP-16)
4. Swappable NPC parts (CAP-9)
5. Town draft (CAP-14)
6. Improved re-collected abilities (CAP-17)

Must-tier fallback: the single-phase demon becomes a simpler boss. If time is short, ship CAP-17 before CAP-19, because they overlap. Temples are optional inside epic-world-worth-exploring and are the first thing cut there.
